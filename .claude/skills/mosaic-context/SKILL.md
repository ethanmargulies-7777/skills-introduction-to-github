# Mosaic Platforms — Company Context

## What Mosaic Is

Mosaic Platforms, Inc. is a privately held fintech startup that operates a U.S. equity **Alternative Trading System (ATS)**. It launched in Q1 2026. The company's thesis: traditional price-time priority matching creates adverse outcomes for institutional investors (information leakage, price slippage, toxicity). Mosaic replaces this with **compatibility-based matching**.

**Investors:** Guggenheim Securities and RBC made strategic investments. Technology powered by **KX** (the high-performance time-series data platform used in institutional finance).

## The MERIT System

**MERIT** = Mosaic's proprietary scoring and matching framework.

- Participants are segmented into two roles: **investors** (buy-side, long-horizon) and **risk providers** (broker-dealers, market makers).
- Each participant receives a MERIT score evaluating how their trading activity affects counterparties — specifically slippage, signaling risk, and information leakage.
- Orders are matched on **compatibility** between investor and risk provider, not solely price-time priority.
- Goal: reduce toxic flow, reward participants who contribute to a healthy market ecosystem.

This is a direct challenge to the dominant dark pool model and a philosophical challenge to Reg NMS-era assumptions about market structure.

## What This Means for Your Internship

Mosaic is pre-scale and early-stage. As an intern you are close to decision-makers. Key areas to understand:

- **Market microstructure** — how orders move from buy-side to execution, what dark pools do, why ATS market share matters
- **Adverse selection** — the core problem Mosaic is solving; know it cold
- **Reg NMS** — the SEC regulation governing U.S. equity trading, order routing, best execution
- **NBBO** — National Best Bid and Offer; the price benchmark all ATS must trade at or better
- **Dark vs. lit markets** — Mosaic is a dark pool (off-exchange); understand the tradeoffs
- **KX/kdb+** — the tech stack; familiarity signals technical credibility

## Talking Points You Should Know

- Why price-time priority alone is insufficient for institutional investors
- How Mosaic's MERIT scoring differs from IEX's speed bump approach (both tackle toxicity, different mechanisms)
- The SEC's ATS-N disclosure regime (Form ATS-N) — Mosaic filed this; it's public on SEC EDGAR
- Guggenheim and RBC as strategic partners — not just financial backers, they're likely early flow providers
