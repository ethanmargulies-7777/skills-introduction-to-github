Context
Why Compatibility-Based Matching Matters
The structural failure modes current venues haven't solved
  • Price-time priority optimizes the match — not what happens after
  • Toxic flow causes post-trade price reversion against the passive side
  • Broker-operated dark pools have structural conflicts enforcing their own controls
  • Barclays LX: claimed "Liquidity Profiling" toxicity controls were fraudulently misrepresented → $154M settlement (2016)
  • As institutional liquidity is internalized, remaining dark pool flow becomes increasingly adversely selected
  • MERIT scores post-trade workflow impact — not just execution mechanics

Venue Deep-Dive — 1 of 4
IEX — Technology-Based Defense
Registered exchange since 2016. No Form ATS-N filed.
Matching
Price-time (displayed); midpoint peg non-displayed; D-Peg retracts when Signal fires, M-Peg stays
Participants
Open access — any registered BD; HFTs permitted but speed-bumped
Fee
$0.0010/share non-displayed (both sides charged); added maker-taker rebates July 2023 reversing prior stance
Toxicity Controls
350µs speed bump (38mi coiled fiber) + CQI Signal (9-rule model, SEC-filed) + D-Limit repricing
Key signal: IEX's Signal fires BEFORE inbound orders arrive — matching engine has real-time data during the delay window.
Watch out: Reversed anti-rebate position under competitive pressure in 2023
Venue Deep-Dive — 2 of 4
Liquidnet — Natural Block Negotiation
Acquired by TP ICAP (March 2021, $700M). Two ATSs: Negotiation + H2O.
Matching
OMS blotter-scraping → indication match → bilateral negotiation window → execution at negotiated price (midpoint, VWAP cross, or agreed price)
Min Size / Participants
5,000 shares or 5% ADV · Buy-side only, ≥$100M AUM · HFTs + prop explicitly excluded · BD carve-out for transition managers only
Fee
Commission per share; no membership fee; no maker-taker
H2O ATS
Auto-execution layer with Liquidity Partners (sell-side BDs) — LP interaction is opt-in only for Members
Watch out: 2025 SEC enforcement $5M (Market Access Rule; confidential subscriber info leakage) · 2014 enforcement $2M (ECM employees accessed OMS data)
Venue Deep-Dive — 3 of 4
BIDS — Behavioral Scoring at Scale
Acquired by Cboe Global Markets 2021. Largest independent block ATS.
Matching
Conditional → private Invitation to Trade → firm-up → midpoint execution; AIOIs for sponsor-targeted bilateral flow
Participants
Two-tier — Subscribers (registered BDs) + Sponsored Firms (buy-side via Sponsor BD); 520+ asset managers, 100+ BDs
Fee
$0.0003–$0.005/share; $100/month port fee capped at $1k/month; no rebates; charged to Subscribers only
Toxicity Controls
BIDS Scorecard (firm-up rate + flow patterns) + client-controlled counterparty filters + no broadcast IOIs
Scale: Jan 2026 ADV = 241M matched shares/day · avg block = 23,604 shares · FY2025 ADV up 96% YoY
Key risk to Mosaic: Biggest competitive threat for block flow — growing aggressively under Cboe

Luminex — Buy-Side Owns the Pool
Founded 2015 by 9 asset managers. Merged with LeveL ATS 2021. Rebranded LeveL Markets April 2025.
Matching
Quantity-then-time priority; midpoint-only pricing; Firm, Conditional, and Negotiable order types
Participants
Buy-side only; ≥$1B AUM preferred; HFTs explicitly excluded; Fidelity ~60% ownership stake
Fee
25–170 mills/share ($0.0025–$0.0175); base ~50 mills; driven by connectivity cost; no maker-taker
Founding Owners
Fidelity · BlackRock · T. Rowe Price · State Street · BNY Mellon · Capital Group · Invesco · JPMorgan AM · MFS
Watch out: Rebranded to LeveL Markets April 2025 — recent consolidation may shift buy-side-only character · Toxicity controls: consortium ownership + $1B AUM gatekeeping + fall-down policing on Conditionals
The Thesis
Mosaic Platforms — Compatibility-Based Matching
Launched April 27, 2026. Backed by Guggenheim Securities + RBC. Powered by KX/kdb+.
  • Matching: MERIT score-driven compatibility matching between Investors (buy-side) and Risk Providers (dealers/MMs)
  • MERIT measures: Post-trade price impact of your workflow on counterparties — slippage, signaling risk, information leakage
  • Unlike BIDS Scorecard: MERIT scores the ongoing trading relationship, not just firm-up behavior
  • Unlike Luminex/Liquidnet: Admits mixed participants but scores and matches by compatibility — doesn't just exclude
  • Unlike IEX: Post-trade behavioral assessment vs. pre-trade technical signal
  • Powered by KX: kdb+ time-series technology enables high-velocity MERIT score computation
  • Status: Conceptually strongest model; no published performance data yet — proving it is the mission

What to Know Before Day One
Six things John Cosenza will likely probe on
  • Barclays is the cautionary case: broker-operated toxicity controls have structural conflicts — Mosaic's external MERIT score and non-BD structure directly addresses this
  • BIDS is the biggest competitive threat: 241M shares/day ADV, up 96% YoY, Cboe distribution, growing fast
  • Luminex/LeveL rebrand (April 2025): buy-side-only model facing consolidation pressure — worth monitoring
  • IEX reversed on rebates (July 2023): even mission-driven venues face commercial realities that compromise the model
  • Brugler & Comerton-Forde (JFE 2025): causal empirical evidence that restricted-access dark pools produce less information leakage — strongest academic validation for what Mosaic is doing
  • VPIN (Easley et al. 2012): the academic ancestor of MERIT — know it cold
